FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Buryit free?

Yes — no account, no subscription.

Which browsers work?

Chrome, Edge, and Brave — all Chromium-based.

Does it read my browsing history?

No. It reads nothing automatically; you type the item and price.

Do I need the extension to use the website?

No. The website works standalone; the extension just brings it one click closer to checkout.

Where is my data stored?

Locally on your device via localStorage. Nothing leaves your browser unless you configure a backend.

Can I override the verdict?

Yes. Buryit is friction, not a lock.

Is this financial advice?

No.

How do I uninstall it?

Via your browser's extensions page; all local data clears with it.
